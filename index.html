Your speakers play a tone too high to hear. Your mic listens to it bounce off your hand. Move towards the screen and the echo goes up in pitch; move away and it drops. That's enough to read gestures. No camera.

  1. 01 built-in speakers + mic
  2. 02 start sonar, keep still
  3. 03 wave 5–15 cm above the keys
  4. 04 control the deck or your mac

    04 / tune
    
        — kHz
      
    
      
      
    Higher is harder to hear but some speakers fade above 19 kHz. If you can hear a whine, go higher. Dogs and cats can hear it.
    
      
      
      
      
    
        
      
    
      
    Recalibrates by itself when you change the tone or volume. Set the trigger so the dashed line sits above the meter when you're still.

python3 bridge.py
